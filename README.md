# 🎬 Stranger Things

## Overview
**Stranger Things** is a popular American science fiction horror drama television series created by the *Duffer Brothers*. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, the show follows the mysterious disappearance of a young boy and the appearance of a girl with psychokinetic abilities.

---

## 🌟 Key Details
* **Genre:** Sci-Fi, Horror, Drama, Mystery
* **Creators:** The Duffer Brothers
* **Original Network:** Netflix
* **Setting:** 1980s Hawkins, Indiana

---

## 🎭 Main Characters
* **Eleven (El)** – A young girl with telekinetic powers.
* **Mike Wheeler** – A dedicated friend who helps hide Eleven.
* **Dustin Henderson** – The witty and clever member of the group.
* **Lucas Sinclair** – The pragmatic and brave voice of reason.
* **Will Byers** – The boy whose disappearance starts it all.

---

2. **Compelling Storyline:** A great balance of supernatural mystery, friendship, and coming-of-age drama.
